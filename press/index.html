📰 Press kit

Soft Skills Kids is a free YouTube channel and website with happy sing-along cartoon songs that teach children aged 3–8 the social and emotional skills that matter most — skills no machine can replace.

📌 Quick facts

  • A new song every few days — each one teaches one skill: teamwork, kindness, feelings, listening, empathy, perseverance, problem solving, confidence, calming down, communication, friendship, respect and more
  • In English and in Greek, with karaoke lyrics on screen
  • Free for families and schools: colouring pages, activity sheets, a picture quiz and a printable certificate for every song
  • Safe by design: no ads on the website, no sign-up, no cookies, no tracking; videos are marked “Made for Kids”
  • Six recurring characters — Max, Mia, Jay, Lily, Rosie and Buddy the puppy — so children recognise their friends in every song

💡 Why we make it

In a world full of technology, the most human skills — understanding feelings, working together, being kind, calming down, solving problems — matter more than ever. Music is the easiest way for young children to learn and remember them, and every child should have free access to these songs, at home or in class.

🎨 How it is made

Soft Skills Kids is created by a parent in Greece. The songs, lyrics and lessons are written and curated by the creator; music and illustrations are produced with the help of digital creative tools, and every song is reviewed for age-appropriate, positive content before it is published.
