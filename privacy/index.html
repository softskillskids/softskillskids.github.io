🔒 Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

The short version

This website does not use cookies, does not track you, has no accounts or sign-up, and does not collect any personal information. It is made to be safe for children and families.

The website

The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The quizzes run entirely in your browser and nothing you answer is stored or sent anywhere.

Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not have access to individual visitor data.

Videos are only loaded when you press play, using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Once a video plays, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Our YouTube channel and tools

We use Google’s YouTube APIs only to upload and manage videos on our own channels and to read our own channels’ aggregated statistics. We do not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users. Our use of these APIs follows the YouTube Terms of Service and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you. We never sell or share email addresses, and if you ask us not to contact you again, we won’t.

Children

Our content is for children aged 3–8 and their parents and teachers. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Our videos are marked “Made for Kids” on YouTube.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at softskillskids1@gmail.com.
