Feelings Activity for Kids: Name It, Draw It, Talk

A song-based activity for ages 3–8. Practise naming feelings and recognising that people may feel differently in the same situation. Includes links to free classroom printables.

Adult-led · Ages 3–8 · Allow about 15–20 minutes and adapt to your group.

Activity goal

Practise naming feelings and recognising that people may feel differently in the same situation.

What you need

Paper and crayons.

Open song & printables

Try the activity

  1. Ask

    How might a character feel on their first day somewhere new? Accept different possible feelings.

  2. Listen

    Play My Feelings Have a Name. Recall a few feeling words together afterwards.

  3. Try

    Draw a feeling for an imaginary character, or point to an example. Children do not need to share personal experiences.

  4. Reflect

    Ask: What might help this character? Explore asking a trusted adult, taking a pause or requesting company. No one action fixes every feeling.

A question to explore

Could two people feel differently about the same event?

Adapt it

Begin with two feeling words for younger children. Older children can suggest mixed feelings or words in their home language.

Optional: do the picture quiz on the song page together. Completion certificates are keepsakes, not assessed qualifications.
