Kindness Activity for Preschool: Small Helpful Actions

A song-based activity for ages 3–8. Notice when someone might appreciate help and practise offering it. Includes links to free classroom printables.

Adult-led · Ages 3–8 · Allow about 15–20 minutes and adapt to your group.

Activity goal

Notice when someone might appreciate help and practise offering it.

What you need

Paper, crayons and an optional soft toy.

Open song & printables

Try the activity

  1. Ask

    What is one small thing someone did to help you? Children may pass.

  2. Listen

    Play Kindness Makes the World Shine. Choose one helpful action to discuss.

  3. Try

    Pretend a toy has dropped its crayons. Model: Would you like some help? Act out possible responses and respect a no.

  4. Reflect

    Draw one kind action children could choose today. Do not require a public promise or rank children.

A question to explore

How could you help someone feel included without making them join in?

Adapt it

Offer gestures or picture choices for children learning English. Older children can create a pretend-play situation.

Optional: do the picture quiz on the song page together. Completion certificates are keepsakes, not assessed qualifications.
