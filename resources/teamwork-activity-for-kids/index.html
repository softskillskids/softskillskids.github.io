Teamwork Activity for Kids: Sing, Build and Share

A song-based activity for ages 3–8. Practise sharing a task and asking a partner for help. Includes links to free classroom printables.

Adult-led · Ages 3–8 · Allow about 15–20 minutes and adapt to your group.

Activity goal

Practise sharing a task and asking a partner for help.

What you need

Paper cups or building blocks.

Open song & printables

Try the activity

  1. Ask

    What can two people make together that is harder to make alone? Children may answer, point or draw.

  2. Listen

    Play We Can Do It Together and notice examples of helping.

  3. Try

    In pairs, build a small tower. Alternate placing a cup or block. Model the phrase: Can you help me?

  4. Reflect

    Ask: What did your partner do that helped? Name a specific action rather than choosing a best team.

A question to explore

What could you say if your partner has a different idea?

Adapt it

For younger children, model two turns. Older children can agree on a plan before building.

Optional: do the picture quiz on the song page together. Completion certificates are keepsakes, not assessed qualifications.
