A happy teamwork song for kids! 🤝 Sing along with Max, Mia, Jay, Lily and Buddy the puppy as they build a treehouse together — and learn that when we help each other, we can do anything!

"We Can Do It Together" is a fun sing-along kids song about teamwork, sharing, helping friends and never giving up. With big karaoke lyrics on screen, it's perfect for preschool, kindergarten and early elementary kids (ages 3–8), at home or in the classroom.

💡 Why this skill matters: teamwork

Children who learn to work together listen to others, share ideas and solve problems as a team. They will need this at school, in friendships and later at work — and in a world full of technology, cooperation stays one of the most human and valuable skills. Give your child small shared “missions” at home and praise the team’s effort, not just the result.

👨‍👩‍👧 What your child learns

  • Teamwork — together we can build bigger dreams
  • Sharing ideas — everyone's ideas matter
  • Mistakes are okay — friends help each other through
  • Growth mindset — if something doesn't work, try another way and never give up

💬 Talk about it after the song

  • When did you help a friend?
  • What could we build together?
  • What do you do when something doesn't work?
