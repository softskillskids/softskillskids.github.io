🍎 For teachers: SEL songs for your classroom

Every song teaches one social-emotional skill in about four minutes, with karaoke lyrics on screen. Everything is free — no sign-up, no ads, no cookies — and works on a classroom screen or projector.

⏱️ A 10-minute lesson with any song

1 min

Warm-up

Ask one question about the skill: “What do you do when a friend is sad?”

4 min

Watch & sing

Play the video. The second time, let the children sing the chorus and do the actions.

3 min

Talk

Use the two questions under “Talk about it after the song” on each song page.

2 min

Quiz

Do the 6-question picture quiz together — the class points, you click. Print a class certificate!
